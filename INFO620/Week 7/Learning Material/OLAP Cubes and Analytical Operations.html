Week 7 Data Warehousing, Dimensional Modeling, and OLAP

OLAP Cubes and Analytical Operations

Online analytical processing (OLAP) systems enable business users to explore multidimensional data dynamically.

OLAP cubes are built on top of dimensional models and support operations that allow users to drill into, reorganize, and summarize data.

OLAP Cube Components

Measures

OLAP cube quantitative metrics include measures such as the following:

Profit Revenue Inventory levels

Dimensions

OLAP cube attributes used for grouping or filtering include dimensions such as the following:

Region Time period Product category

Hierarchies

OLAP cubes use hierarchies—that is, ordered levels, such as the following:

  • Year → Quarter → Month → Day
  • Country → State → City

OLAP Operations

OLAP operations include the following:

  1. Roll-up (aggregation): Aggregates data to a higher level.
    Example: Month → Quarter
  2. Drill-down (disaggregation): Moves from summary to detail.
    Example: Region → Individual Stores
  3. Slice: Filters data on one dimension.
    Example: Sales for 2024 only
  4. Dice: Filters data on two or more dimensions.
    Example: Sales for 2024 in Region = West AND Category = Electronics
  5. Pivot (rotate): Reorients the cube to provide different perspectives.

OLAP Types

There are a few OLAP types:

  • MOLAP: Cube stored in multidimensional structures
  • ROLAP: Cube simulated through relational tables
  • HOLAP: Hybrid approach

ROLAP is common with modern SQL-based warehouses.
