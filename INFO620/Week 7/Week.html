What to Expect in Week 7

Week 7 transitions from transactional database systems into the realm of analytical systems, where substantial volumes of historical data are stored, processed, and analyzed to facilitate informed decision-making. Unlike OLTP systems optimized for rapid transactional updates, data warehouses (DWs) are engineered for query efficiency, historical data retention, and complex analytical processes. You will examine dimensional modeling, star and snowflake schemas, OLAP operations, and the architectural challenges encountered by organizations when establishing data warehousing environments. A comprehensive understanding of these concepts is vital for careers in business intelligence, analytics engineering, and enterprise data architecture.
