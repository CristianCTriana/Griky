Bienvenido a ISSC621: Informática Forense

Welcome to Computer Forensics, where you’re going to learn about digital forensics and the way it actually works in real investigations—not as a collection of cool tools, but as a disciplined process that has to hold up under scrutiny.

Over the next eight weeks, you’ll build the mindset and the workflow that forensic professionals rely on when evidence can’t be guessed, rushed, or improvised. Because in digital forensics, it’s not enough to “find something interesting.” You have to be able to explain what you did, prove that you didn’t alter the evidence, and show that your results are reliable—especially if they ever end up in court.

This course is designed to take you from the fundamentals to real-world practice. You’ll start by defining what digital forensics is, what counts as evidence, and why chain of custody matters so much. Then you’ll explore different types of internet crimes and the kinds of evidence they leave behind. You’ll learn the basics of computer electronics and the investigation tools used in forensic work. You’ll practice managing a crime scene—because digital evidence doesn’t exist in a vacuum;, it’s part of a bigger investigative context.

As the course progresses, you’ll dig into disk and file structures, which is where a lot of forensic truth lives—what is stored, what is deleted, what is hidden, and what can be recovered. You’ll explore what a digital forensics lab does, why labs operate with strict procedures, and how quality control affects credibility. Then you’ll move into mobile forensics, because phones are often the most personal and evidence-rich devices in an investigation—and also some of the most complex to handle responsibly. Finally, you’ll bring it all together with reporting and court-focused communication: how you present findings clearly, how you document your process, and how you make your work stand up under questions.

Each week follows a consistent structure to keep you moving from understanding to application. You’ll get:

  • a week overview to frame what matters most,
  • two short coach videos to guide your thinking,
  • an infographic that gives you a clear visual map,
  • a realistic case story to practice analysis,
  • a self-assessment practice activity with feedback so you can check your understanding,
  • and a podcast-style lesson that connects everything in a natural, conversational way.

By the end of Computer Forensics, you won’t just know what digital forensics is—you’ll be able to think like a forensics professional. You’ll know how to preserve evidence, how to document your actions, how to use tools responsibly, and how to communicate findings in a way that’s credible, defensible, and clear.
