Qué esperar en la Semana 1

Esta semana se le presentarán los conceptos, perspectivas y marcos de referencia de la informática forense. Explorará la informática forense y la evidencia digital, examinará las diferencias entre HIPAA, SOX y COPPA, cómo estas leyes se relacionan con el delito informático, y obtendrá una comprensión de la necesidad de órdenes judiciales para la persecución de delitos informáticos.

Bienvenidos a la Semana 1

Transcripción: Bienvenido a la Semana 1 +

You’re starting Week 1: An Introduction to Digital Forensics, and this week is about building the foundation you’ll use for every investigation that follows.

You’ll learn what digital forensics actually is, and what it is not. It’s not just “recover deleted files” or “run a tool and read the results.” Digital forensics is a disciplined process for identifying, preserving, collecting, examining, and reporting digital evidence in a way that is credible and defensible.

You’ll also focus on the concepts that make forensic work trustworthy: integrity, repeatability, and chain of custody. That means understanding why evidence handling matters, what can contaminate evidence, and how documentation protects both the investigation and you as the investigator.

Suggested visuals/scenes: definition graphics, evidence lifecycle timeline, chain-of-custody form visuals, “do not alter” stamp, simple lab/field scenes.

By the end of this week, you’ll be able to explain the digital forensics lifecycle and describe how evidence moves from discovery to analysis to reporting—without breaking integrity along the way.
