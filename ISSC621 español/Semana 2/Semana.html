Qué esperar en la Semana 2

Esta semana aprenderá sobre los diversos delitos e incidentes involucrados en las investigaciones de informática forense electrónica. Analizará los distintos tipos de evidencia que pueden recopilarse para cada categoría de delito. También explorará la importancia de las políticas de seguridad y uso de computadoras para comprender delitos informáticos como el robo de identidad, el fraude en Internet y las estafas.

Bienvenidos a la Semana 2

Transcripción: Bienvenidos a la Semana 2 +

Welcome to Week 2: Types of Internet Crimes & Evidence. This week, you’re going to connect two things that always belong together in digital forensics: the crime and the trace it leaves behind. 

You’ll explore common categories of internet crime—like phishing, identity theft, online fraud, unauthorized access, malware-enabled theft, harassment, and data misuse—and you’ll learn how each one creates different kinds of digital footprints. You’ll also focus on what counts as evidence in these cases: emails and headers, browser artifacts, login histories, IP and network logs, cloud activity, messaging records, payment and transaction indicators, and timestamps that help you build a timeline you can defend. 

You’ll practice thinking like an investigator: where to look first, what to preserve before it disappears, and how to avoid “jumping to conclusions” when attribution is unclear. 

By the end of this week, you’ll be able to recognize major internet crime patterns and explain what evidence is most relevant—and why.
