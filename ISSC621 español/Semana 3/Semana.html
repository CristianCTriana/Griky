Qué esperar en la Semana 3

¿Cuáles son los principales desafíos en la identificación y recolección de evidencia forense electrónica proveniente de diversos componentes de hardware y software? ¿Cómo influyen los estándares éticos y los marcos legales en la selección de metodologías y herramientas forenses al manejar evidencia digital? Proporcione ejemplos de cómo estos principios se aplican en investigaciones forenses cibernéticas del mundo real y evalúe críticamente las implicaciones para mantener la integridad de la evidencia a través de múltiples escenas electrónicas.

Bienvenidos a la Semana 3

Transcripción: Bienvenidos a la Semana 3 +

Welcome to Week 3: Computer Electronics & Investigations Tools. This week, you’re going to connect the “what” of digital forensics to the “how.” Because before you can interpret evidence, you need to understand how digital devices actually store, move, and reveal information—and why your tool choices matter. 

You’ll look at the basics of computer electronics in a way that supports investigations: how storage works at a high level, what memory does, why power state matters, and how devices can retain traces even after someone tries to delete or hide them. You’ll also explore investigation tools and why professional workflows rely on controlled methods—like write protection, verified acquisition, hashing, and repeatable analysis. 

This week also helps you think like a forensic practitioner: you’re not choosing tools because they’re popular. You’re choosing tools because they support integrity, minimize contamination, and produce results you can explain and defend.  

By the end of Week 3, you’ll be able to explain why hardware and system state matter—and how forensic tools fit into a defensible investigation process.
