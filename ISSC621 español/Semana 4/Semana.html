Qué esperar en la Semana 4

Descripción General:

Esta semana comprenderás los requisitos para la gestión de la escena del incidente/crimen. Identificarás los pasos necesarios para que la evidencia electrónica sea admisible en un tribunal. Revisarás los procedimientos para identificar, recolectar y proteger la evidencia electrónica.

Bienvenidos a la Semana 4

Transcripción: Bienvenidos a la Semana 4 +

Welcome to Week 4: Managing the Crime Scene. This week is about what happens before you ever run a tool or open a report—because the scene is where evidence can be preserved… or destroyed. 

You’ll learn how to control an investigation environment, whether it’s a physical room with devices, a workplace workstation area, a home office, or even a distributed “scene” that includes cloud accounts and remote systems. You’ll focus on the basics that make evidence credible: securing the area, limiting access, documenting what you see, identifying potential evidence sources, and maintaining a chain of custody from the first moment. 

You’ll also practice practical decision-making: when to photograph or document, when to isolate devices from networks, how to handle powered-on systems responsibly, and how to coordinate with stakeholders without letting “helpful” people contaminate the investigation. 

By the end of this week, you’ll be able to describe what good scene management looks like—and why your first actions determine whether the evidence will stand up later.
