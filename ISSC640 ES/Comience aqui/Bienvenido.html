NARRATOR: Every message you send, every video you stream, every system you rely on travels through a network you can't see but must understand. Welcome to Computer Networks and Data Systems, a course that takes you inside the technologies shaping today's connected world.

You'll explore how data actually moves, from physical connections and packet switching to protocols like TCP, IP, UDP, and IPv6, building a clear understanding of how modern networks are designed and managed.

Along the way, you'll analyze network architectures, compare LANs, VANs, and wireless systems, and evaluate performance, scalability, reliability, and security across real-world environments. You'll also tackle today's most pressing challenges, cloud services, internet telephony, multimedia networks, emerging trends like AI and IoT, and the decisions organizations face when designing communication systems.

This isn't just theory. You'll design enterprise and wireless networks, assess communication systems holistically, and apply what you learn to real organizational needs. By the end of this course, you won't just know how networks work. You'll understand why they work, how they evolve, and how to design them for the future.
