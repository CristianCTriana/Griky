ISSC642 Course Overview

Scope, Learning Objectives, and Assessment

| Course Scope

Students will explore network-based and host-based intrusion detection systems, attack recognition patterns, risk analysis, forensic data collection, and the application of artificial intelligence in threat detection. Real-world scenarios and modern network monitoring tools are discussed to reinforce applied knowledge. The course also reviews the various leadership roles in a security team and organizational complexity as it applies to working with intrusion detection.
