Week 1 Introduction to Intrusion Detection & Incident Handling

Introduction

This lesson introduces the CIA Triad—Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability—as the core framework guiding modern cybersecurity. You'll also learn how different control types, from administrative policies to technical safeguards and physical protections, work together to prevent, detect, and correct threats while helping organizations classify and manage risks using a systematic risk taxonomy.

As mentioned, preventative control measures involve fortifying defenses to stop incidents before they happen. Two categories of controls include timing-based and nature-based:

Timing-based Controls:
  • Detective controls: intrusion and detection monitoring that alerts stake holders to problems
  • Corrective Controls: building in resilience to restore systems that have been compromised
Nature-based Controls:
  • Administrative: “policies, procedures, and governance measures” that regulate and guide cybersecurity within an organization. This includes the required training.
  • Technical: Applications that inhibit threats and detect those that come through- includes encryption, firewalls, and multi-factor authentication.
  • Physical: Security equipment used to block intruder access. This includes protecting equipment and servers in secure, fortified locations, monitoring with security cameras and tools to ensure only authorized users can access systems.
