The Control Lifecycle

Controls need to adapt to organizational changes as well as external threats. As shown in the following diagram, the control cycle is:

  • Starts with Identification and Selection of controls,
  • followed by Design,
  • Implementation,
  • Maintenance and Improvement,
  • and finally Decommissioning and Replacement when necessary (Edwards, 2025).
