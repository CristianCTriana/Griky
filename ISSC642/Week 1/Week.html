What to Expect in Week 1

This week we examine why information protection teams are needed, the roles of cybersecurity leadership in organizations, as well as government requirements for those working in the cybersecurity industry. We will get started with some key concepts and definitions, before moving into a more specific discussion of policies.

Welcome to Week 1

Objectives

CO-1: Examine the security roles involved with intrusion detection. ▼
  • LO-1.1: Describe the fundamental need for an information protection team.
  • LO-1.2: Analyze the roles of cybersecurity leadership and frameworks in organizations.
