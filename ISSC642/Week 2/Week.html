What to Expect in Week 2

This week, we will observe how incident handling and its reporting provide us with information as to what is working properly and what needs to be tended to in our networks. Although there is no foolproof method for this practice, there are resources that can help us in this process. Thus, let’s begin by reviewing the MITRE ATT&CK CVEs.
