What to Expect in Week 7

One of the best planning methods is wishing for the best but preparing for the worst. As we are nearing the end of the course, we need to be sure we understand how to implement a proper incident resource planning procedure. If customers are relying on our data and/or online services, we could be held legally responsible for the damage they may cause to our customers. Therefore, let’s observe how to implement this planning and which measures we should be practicing.
