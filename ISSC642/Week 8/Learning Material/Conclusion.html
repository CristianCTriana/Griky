Week 8
Future Intrusions and Threats

Conclusions

In summary, Artificial Intelligence represents a transformative "double-edged sword" within the realm of intrusion detection, offering defenders unprecedented capabilities in behavioral anomaly detection and predictive analysis. By establishing baselines of normal network activity, AI can identify subtle deviations and correlate minor events across vast datasets to intercept attacks before they fully manifest. Furthermore, its ability to automate triage and analyze encrypted traffic reduces alert fatigue for human analysts, shifting the focus toward "Augmented Intelligence" where machine speed is balanced by human intuition and contextual understanding.

However, these same advancements have ignited a high-stakes AI arms race, as adversaries weaponize machine learning to bypass modern defenses. Attackers utilize adversarial AI to create "adversarial examples" and polymorphic malware that evade detection models, while employing model poisoning to trick systems into ignoring malicious behavior. As criminal tactics evolve to include automated, highly personalized phishing and data poisoning, it is essential for cybersecurity professionals to recognize that intrusion detection is an ongoing process. Maintaining a resilient posture requires a constant commitment to updating detection methods to counter the increasing volume and sophistication of AI-driven exploits.
