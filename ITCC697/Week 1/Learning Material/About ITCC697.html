Course Overview

About ITCC697

The course is designed as a practical exercise for IT professionals. Over the next 16 weeks, you will be called upon to take what you have learned and tie it all together. You will leverage your previous coursework to create a practical deliverable.

Upon successful completion of the course, you have will have three usable artifacts:

  • A project proposal
  • A business case
  • A “pitch” deck

The course is 16 weeks long. Your creative capstone forms the actual content of the course. The three artifacts mentioned above are the chief deliverables of the course and provide the primary means of assessment.

Throughout the course there are weekly assignments, quizzes, and various knowledge checks.

Weekly check-ins in the form of discussion posts are required, along with specific discussion topics.

Critical Success Factors

McKinsey undertook a large study across 5,400 projects. (Bloch et al., 2012)

They stated four critical success factors for project success:

  1. Management of strategy and stakeholders over budget and schedule
  2. Mastery of technology and project content
  3. Effectively aligning teams
  4. Excelling at core project management practices

The three artifacts you produce in this course will assist you with these four Critical Success Factors and aid you in bringing your project to life.

Hence This Course

Some would say that “The best idea wins.” This simply isn’t true.

“Nothing happens until a sale is made.”—Thomas J. Watson, Sr.

You must be able to “sell” your ideas to others. That is, you have concrete experience from your viewpoint, but you must be able to share that experience with others in order to bring them around to your point of view. You must master the details of a given problem, conceptualize an appropriate solution, and enlist others to support that solution.

All four of McKinsey’s critical success factors involve socialization of ideas. That is, selling. You get the execs on board, the team engaged, leverage their talents, and execute.

This course is about organizing your ideas and doing your homework so you can sell your great idea to others. It’s not about a document or a set of documents, it’s about the mindset you need to demonstrate to sell your great idea.
