Assessments

You will have three assessments in this course. They are summarized below.

Assessment 1: A Project Proposal

  • A short three- to five-page document that outlines a project, its details, objectives, and approach at a high level.
  • It includes: an executive summary, basic background information, high-level objectives, scope, budget estimates, proposed timeline, expected risks, key deliverables, and success criteria.
  • Its purpose is to engage with the stakeholder and gain their buy-in.
  • It answers the basic questions: What is it? What is it for? Why is it important? What do you need to get it done? What do you get out of doing it?

Assessment 2: A Business Case

  • A detailed document of up to 50 pages that provides the justification for pursuing a project including its feasibility, viability, and benefits
  • It explains the problem, discusses alternatives, recommends a solution, and explains how it will be implemented
  • It includes an executive summary, problem statement, detailed analysis of the current state, alternative solutions including cost/benefit analysis, recommended solution, and a proposal for implementation.
  • It provides all the details for answering the basic questions: What is it? What is it for? Why is it important? What do you need to get it done? What do you get out of doing it?

Assessment 3: A “Pitch” Deck

  • A 10 to 20-page PowerPoint deck that gives a quick overview of a proposed project that “sells” the project.
  • It is a very high-level pitch that uses compelling visuals with a clear narrative that presents concise data and information about the project.
  • It includes a statement of the problem, the proposed solution, the benefits of the solution, and the return on investment of the solution.
  • It sells the answers to the basic questions: What is it? What is it for? Why is it important? What do you need to get it done? What do you get out of doing it?

Weekly Posting

In the world of Agile Development, there is a concept of a daily standup. This daily meeting provides the team with alignment, collaboration, and early detection of problems.

In this case, you will be posting weekly in the discussion group.

This is a three-minute task answering three questions:

  • What did you do last week?
  • What are you doing this week?
  • What blockers or issues, if any, are you facing?

Peer Reply Guidance:

  • Read your peer’s update carefully.
  • Comment on what they did and their plans (if applicable).
  • Offer support or ideas if they mention blockers.
