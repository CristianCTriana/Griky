Week 1
Course Overview

Conclusion

This week’s lesson focused on helping you begin to integrate academic research with practical problem-solving. The lesson emphasized applying research methodologies, analyzing scholarly and practitioner approaches, and using graduate-level critical thinking to address real-world IT challenges. Remember, rather than building a fully deployed product, you will develop a viable project proposal and business case that demonstrates strategic thinking, evidence-based decision-making, and relevance to your professional field.

The lesson also introduced you to the scholarly practitioner paradigm by highlighting the value of combining theory and action to improve organizational performance. This lesson and future lessons will further convey how a project’s success depends not only on technical execution but also on stakeholder management, strategic alignment, teamwork, and effective project management. These and other principles, concepts, and theories will draw on industry research for further reinforcements in the upcoming weeks. Additionally, expect a central theme of this course to be helping you clearly communicate and sell ideas to gain stakeholder support, positioning you to make strong presentations and exercise persuasion skills, which are critical success factors for you as an IT professional.

Over the remaining weeks, you will be expected to produce three major deliverables, including a project proposal, a detailed business case, and a pitch deck. These activities will be supported by your weekly assignments, discussions, quizzes, and agile-style progress check-ins.
