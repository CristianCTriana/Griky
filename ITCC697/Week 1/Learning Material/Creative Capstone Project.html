Course Overview

Creative Capstone Project

A capstone may also operate as a keystone, as in an arch. The course is designed to both complete your course of study in your concentration and tie together all the things you have learned.

Congratulations! You Are Here...

Students have moved through a common core curriculum and then diverged into different concentrations. Coming out of this concentration, you are offered a choice: either complete a thesis paper or execute a creative project. ITCC697 is the creative project.

In 16 weeks, there is no expectation that you will be able to design, develop, and deploy a complete product. Instead, the focus shifts to thinking deeply about your idea and developing a viable business case to bring that idea forward.
