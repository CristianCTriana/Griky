Quick Assessment

After reading through this week’s content, complete the optional, ungraded, quick assessment below to check your understanding of the key concepts.

Question: What is the primary purpose of the IT Creative Project Capstone course?

Correct answer: C. To integrate academic knowledge with practical application through a creative project

Feedback: The course is designed to help students address real-world IT issues by applying their academic learning in a practical, project-based format.

Name two differences between the academic and practitioner paradigms as discussed in the lesson.

Academics focus on knowledge and understanding through critical analysis and long-term, controlled experimentation, while practitioners focus on action, implementing changes that work, and achieving efficient, time-sensitive results. 

Feedback: The lesson highlights that the academic paradigm values theory and rigorous analysis, whereas practitioners prioritize practical solutions and immediate performance. 

What is the main benefit of adopting a scholar-practitioner (“pracademic”) approach? 

It enables individuals to use sound theory to guide practical action, ensuring evidence-based decisions that positively impact organizational outcomes. The scholar-practitioner approach bridges theory and practice, promoting actions grounded in research and evidence while solving real-world problems.

List the four critical success factors for project success identified by McKinsey.

(1) Management of strategy and stakeholders over budget and schedule; (2) Mastery of technology and project content; (3) Effectively aligning teams; (4) Excelling at core project management practices. 

Feedback: Recognizing these factors is important for aligning your project work with industry best practices and ensuring success.
