Two Paradigms

Following McNatt et al. (2010), there are two paradigms that exist within the world and provide very different views of the world: Scholars and practitioners. They are different.

Academic

  • Knowledge and understanding as focus
  • Critical analysis and synthesis to understand phenomena
  • Rigorous, controlled experimentation
  • Long-term focus taking years to develop expertise
  • Produces new knowledge

Practitioner

  • Action as focus
  • Making changes that work
  • Bottom-line performance
  • Time sensitive
  • Enhanced efficiency, productivity, or profits
