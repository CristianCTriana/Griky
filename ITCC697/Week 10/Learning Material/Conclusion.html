Week 10
Cost/Benefit/ROI

Conclusion

The financial considerations of your project are important to recognize and capture. Working in conjunction with a Finance partner within your organization, you should be able to craft a sound, conservative, and verifiable cost/benefit/ROI picture for your project.

This module has covered a simple calculation for arriving at the overall project benefit using financial terms. Your organization may have different definitions as well as specific requirements for depicting financial benefits.

Ideally, the calculations here would factor into project evaluations as the end of the implementation phase and provide a clear benchmark for financial success.
