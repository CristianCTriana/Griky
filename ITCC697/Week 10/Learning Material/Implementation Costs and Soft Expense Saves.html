Cost/Benefit/ROI

Implementation Costs and Soft Expense Saves

Implementation Costs

Definition: Incremental direct costs to implement the recommended solutions or increases to recurring costs that result.


Examples (partial list):

  • Costs to procure and install capital equipment, systems and leasehold improvements.
  • Increased postage costs due to adopting express mail versus regular mail to reduce cycle time.
  • Includes: Employee separation costs, if applicable.
  • Does NOT include: Notional value of team members’ time.

Soft Expense Saves

Definition: Productivity enhancements, resources freed, or costs avoided that do not directly impact net income.


Examples (partial list):

  • Reallocation of resources from non-value-added activities to value-added, without a net reduction in FTE.
  • Office space freed up where the leasehold is not relinquished or subleased to another tenant.
  • Increases in customer satisfaction that cannot be directly tied to increased business.
  • Difficult to measure or isolate to a specific cost center, yet are important and beneficial to the business.
  • Costs avoided not previously incurred or whose future incidence is uncertain.
