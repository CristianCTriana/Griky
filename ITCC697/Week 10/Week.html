| Week 10 Relevance: Cost/Benefit/ROI

Week 10 connects financial thinking directly to how IT projects gain approval and demonstrate value. The lesson shows that good ideas alone are not enough—successful projects must be explained in the “language of finance,” because organizations ultimately make decisions based on money and measurable outcomes. Concepts such as incremental revenue, hard expense savings, implementation costs, and soft expense savings work together in a single benefit calculation that translates technical improvements into financial impact. Incremental revenue explains how projects can generate new income, while hard expense saves show permanent cost reductions that directly affect the bottom line. Implementation costs ensure projects are evaluated realistically, and soft saves capture important—but less tangible—benefits like productivity gains and improved customer satisfaction.

These ideas matter because they give you a practical framework for justifying IT initiatives in real organizations. By understanding how benefits are calculated and classified, you will learn how to build conservative, credible business cases that align technical work with organizational goals. The distinction between hard and soft savings helps prevent overstating value, while cost avoidance rules reinforce the importance of evidence and accountability. Together, these concepts will prepare you to collaborate effectively with finance partners, evaluate project success objectively, and communicate the true value of your work to decision makers who control resources and funding.
