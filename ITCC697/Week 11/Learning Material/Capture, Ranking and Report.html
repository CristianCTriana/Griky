Capture, Ranking and Report

Risk Capture and Ranking

A risk register is a tool to capture and manage risks in one place. Often, an organization will have a master list of risks, which is a risk register. Your project may engender new risk for inclusion in that register.

Each project manages its own risk register with input to the central risk team, if such a team exists in your organization.

  • Collection of risks identified
  • Risk owners
  • Risk responses
  • Highest fear factor for failure
  • Not an exhaustive list across all risk dimensions
  • Very low, low
  • Moderate
  • High, very high

A snapshot of the risk register highlighting the key risks and issues is suitable for inclusion in the business case.

Risk Report

Proactively reviewing the risk register mitigates risk. Update it regularly, which the project team and owners of the risks do. This is a very detailed process and is not suitable for executives or sponsors. Generate risk reports for them to consume.

Overall project risk rating

  • Key drivers

Summary information

  • Distribution across categories

A risk report, including an overall project risk assessment in the business case, is appropriate.

Issues

Issues are events that have already occurred and require action to resolve. They are tracked and managed to prevent negative impact on the project.

Events that occur and require action

  • Captured in an issue log
  • Reviewed regularly

Issue Tracking

  • Type of issue
  • Who raised the issue and when
  • Description of the issue
  • Priority
  • Who is responsible to address the issue
  • Target date
  • Status
  • Resolution
