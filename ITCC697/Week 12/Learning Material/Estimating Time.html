Week 12
High-level Project Planning

Estimating Time

Having laid out the basic tasks in chunks, you need to estimate how long each will take. Where do these figures come from?

Historical data

Has similar work been undertaken in the past? How long did it take? There may be after-action reports or project management and audit records that provide this data.

Experience

What have you encountered in the past with regard to projects? While this is anecdotal evidence, it is often a good enough gauge for an estimate.

Subject matter experts

This method involves asking the people who should know, either individually or collectively.

Blended method

Often, doing a combination of the above is the best way forward. Take a historic measure, estimate a difference between that old project and this one, and make a new estimate that you validate with an SME.
