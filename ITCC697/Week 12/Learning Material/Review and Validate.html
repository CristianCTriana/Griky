Week 12
High-level Project Planning

Review and Validate

It is important to remember that people are involved in any project initiative. You will need management support and buy-in, as well as subject matter expertise to develop your business case. Ensure you take the time to socialize your ideas with your stakeholders, especially your project sponsor.

The project sponsor should become your champion, but only if they know what you’re doing. The initial proposal should have their interest. When you meet with them, ask for their input and guidance. Ensure that you carefully weigh their input.

Other stakeholders are equally important, and customers are especially important. However, reaching out to them directly may not be feasible. Instead, rely on proxies for the Voice of the Customer such as help desk logs or complaints.

Reviewing and validating the timeline information with your sponsor and other internal stakeholders goes a long way in garnering support for the idea. It also elicits excellent feedback to fine-tune the business case.
