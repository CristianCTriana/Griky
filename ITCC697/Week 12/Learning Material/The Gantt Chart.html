High-level Project Planning

The Gantt Chart

One of the most useful formats for a high-level project plan is a Gantt chart. Anyone familiar with Microsoft Project will have seen the Gantt Chart View. The Gantt chart is a visual representation of project effort over time.

Below is an example of a Gantt chart view of this course:

How to Read This Timeline

This Gantt-style chart shows how major course tasks unfold over 16 weeks. Click the highlighted areas to learn how it works. You can also tab through. There are four sections to explore: weeks, task, duration, overlapping bars.

  • Tasks are listed on the left
  • Time is listed at the top
  • Horizontal bars indicate duration
  • Bars that occupy the same timeframe happen concurrently

Additional details, such as critical paths and dependencies, may be included if necessary.
