High-level Project Planning

Types of Project Charts

The Gantt chart is one popular method for visually representing project timelines. There are other types such as PERT (Program Evaluation and Review Technique) and CPM (Critical Path Method). However, given the ubiquity of Microsoft on the desktop, Microsoft Project and its Gantt chart view is more common. Your organization may have a specific approach to project timelines.

Note: The Gantt chart is proposed here as a simpler model.
