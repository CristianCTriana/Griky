Week 13
Recommendations

Appearance of a Solution

The recommendation provides one alternative of best fit from an array of choices. The solution traces back to the problem statement: how you have framed the problem or opportunity to begin with.

The problem statement made its first appearance in the proposal. It is a statement about a particular issue.

  • Detailed analysis about that problem statement leads to richer insights about the nature of the problem.
  • These details entail solutions, and different solutions provide the alternatives.
  • Your recommendation comes from those alternatives and states the best solution to the problem.

Essentially, the framing of your problem statement leads you to a particular array of choices.
