Week 13
Recommendations

Conclusion

This module has walked through a recommendation. This recommendation is the key deliverable of the business case. You have noted a problem or opportunity, researched ways of addressing that problem or opportunity, and have analyzed a variety of alternatives. Here, you are stating what you believe to be the appropriate solution.

The recommendation is just that: a recommendation. Ultimately, it is up to the decision-makers to agree to implement that recommendation. In a sense, that is why the business case exists at all: to convince the decision-makers to move ahead with the solution you have presented.

The recommendation isn’t something that you invent; it should follow throughout the business case. The goal of a recommendation is to offer the best possible solution based on your analysis.

The recommendation arises from how you have framed the problem, what you analyzed, and what best fits your stakeholders’ needs.

The recommendation includes goals, targets, and benefits that analysis indicates are the best possible outcomes. Later project success is beyond the scope of the business case. However, any measure of success should be tied to those targets and benefits as stated.
