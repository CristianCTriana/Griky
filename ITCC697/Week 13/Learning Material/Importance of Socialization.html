Recommendations

Importance of Socialization

A recommendation should not be a surprise.

The proposal gains the support of your key stakeholders and sponsors. Once approved by the sponsor and the work begins to develop the business case, the problem statement should be well understood and accepted by all stakeholders.

  • Socialize the proposal among the broader stakeholder community.
  • Ask for feedback on the proposal as well as input.
  • Gain permission from them to engage with subject matter experts.
  • Engage with those SMEs to develop the business case.

Working alone is fine for developing your business case for this course. Working within an organization means working with, through, and for other people. Developing a business case in the “real world” is a team effort.
