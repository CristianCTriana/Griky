Project Success

There are only “vague notions” of project success in the literature (Ika & Pinto, 2023).

You may have a successful project that delivers on time, within budget, and meets deliverables, but adoption falters and benefits never materialize. That highlights the difference between project management success and overall project success.

The business case discusses the benefits of implementing a given recommendation. The recommendation is the best among the options you analyzed.

The traditional view of project success includes the “iron triangle.”

However, more modern interpretations of project success include additional dimensions:

1. Project Management Success

The traditional “iron triangle” (Time, Cost, Quality).

2. Project Ownership Success

Targets and the business case are achieved.

3. Investment Success

Overall success, especially long-term monetary rewards.
