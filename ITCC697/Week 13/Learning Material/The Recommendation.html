Recommendations

The Recommendation

The recommendation is only a few paragraphs at most. It should summarize the information presented in the detailed analysis section. It should:

Restate the goal developed in response to the problem statement

State the recommended action

Summary justification for that action (e.g., favorable cost/benefit)

What key risk is of concern? (Stick to one)

How does the recommendation address that risk?

What are the next steps?
