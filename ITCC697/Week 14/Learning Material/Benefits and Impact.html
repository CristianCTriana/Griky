Week 14
Executive Summary

Benefits and Impact

Here is where you state how the material and non-material benefits you have identified relate to the solution you recommend. It quantifies the “so what” of your recommendation.

Material Impacts

Material impacts are typically data-driven, quantifiable, and readily apparent. Non-material impacts include things like customer satisfaction, employee engagement, or enhancing the organization’s reputation.

Non-Material Impacts

Non-material impacts are harder to quantify, based on sentiment, and often require additional effort to gauge.

Benefits and impacts justify the investment. They outline the tangible changes that the proposal will bring and outline the measurable impacts that result. The benefits and impact provide benchmarks against which to measure the project's success later on.

ROI

Invest this to make that.

Efficiency

Impacts on cost, quality, and cycle time.

Strategic positioning

Alignment with broader organizational goals.

Reduction of risk

Mitigating or eliminating a particular exposure.

Stakeholder Impact

How the project will affect staff, executives, and customers.

Best Practice: The risk and benefit statements are not a laundry list of facts. Rather, they are optimistic estimates of future potential impacts. They should be enticing enough to the reader to encourage support, but not so hyperbolic as to be unreasonable.
