Week 14
Executive Summary

Key Components

Key components are the essential elements every effective executive summary must include, helping writers ensure completeness, clarity, and executive-level focus before drafting the narrative.

Opening Sentence

A strong statement that grabs attention.

Purpose or Problem

A terse statement about what the business case is about.

Key Findings

What is most critical about the business case? What stands out to you from your work?

Recommendation

What do you propose should be done about the problem?

Benefits and Impact

What good comes of doing what you propose?

Cost

How much of an investment is required to get the reward?

Call to Action

Clearly state what next step should be taken.
