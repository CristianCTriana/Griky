Week 14
Executive Summary

Key Findings and Recommendation

Key Findings

Capturing attention with a strong opening followed by the problem statement moves directly toward key findings, including core data or other discoveries.

In the executive summary, these really are the key findings: those facts or evidence that most clearly speak to the heart of the problem or opportunity. Sometimes, your opening sentence may contain the key finding. Relate the sentences on key findings to the opening sentence.

The opening sentence states the “what,” the key findings are the “so what,” why it matters.

Build on your opening. Continue the key findings by showing those key facts that support and underline what you stated in the opening.

Recommendation

The recommendation is the “so what” of the executive summary. In response to the problem or opportunity stated in the opening, bolstered by the key findings, what should be done?

The recommendation is a straightforward statement of the solution.
