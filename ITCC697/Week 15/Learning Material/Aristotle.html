Week 15
Rule of Three

Aristotle

Aristotle discusses three parts of a speech: the introduction (prosthesis) stating what the speech is about, the proof of what you’re saying (pistis), and finally, the closing or epilogue showing that you’ve accomplished what you set out to do (Caicedo, 2011, p.406).

These three equate with Marshall (2019, p.46):

  1. Brief introduction (prosthesis)
  2. Making the case (pistis)
  3. Recap the main points (epilogue)

The point here is not to school you in Greek philosophy, but rather to show you a trite formula that has stood the test of time, more than 2,000 years.
