Rule of Three

Other Rules of Three

“The rule of three” is a simple phrase that appears in many contexts.

In math and statistics, it appears in the calculations of upper bounds of probability (Janovic & Levy, 1997).

In law, the rule of three (Barry, 2018) is used as a rhetorical flourish. This is a technique adopted from other good writing. It appears in many forms, from a single word repeated to triplets of related phrases or sentences.

Marshall (2019) uses it in the same way as Toastmasters International does: a way to lay out a speech. This formula is very old, appearing in Aristotle’s Rhetoric some 2,500 years ago.
