What is a “Pitch” Deck?

If you’ve ever seen the TV show “Shark Tank,” then you have seen pitches in action. Entrepreneurs attempt to entice “the sharks” to invest in their products. You are pitching your idea to your stakeholders. 

The term “pitch” appeared in the U.S. in the early 1900s with travelling salesmen, but became popular in the post-World War II era. With the rise of advertising and the introduction of new media, such as television “pitch men” appeared on late night TV loudly and hyperbolically flogging a product. In the modern era, the internet has disrupted this. We have moved toward the idea of “influencers.” Now, it seems as if everyone is selling something. 

In the context of this course, “pitch” deck is a formal presentation to the stakeholders. There are many formats for a “pitch” deck. Your organization may have its preferred method of presentation. In general, a narrated presentation of slides is standard.
