ITCC697 Course at a Glance: Recap

Here’s what we accomplished throughout this journey: 

Week 1: Course Overview 

  1. Introduction to the capstone process 
  2. Understanding the Scholar–Practitioner / “Pracademic” model 
  3. Overview of the three major course deliverables: 
    1. Proposal 
    2. Business Case 
    3. Pitch Deck 
  4. Weekly discussion check-ins begin 

Week 2: Proposal Creation 

  1. Learn the purpose and structure of a proposal 
  2. Build the A3 (problem, background, current state, analysis, proposal, resources, risks) 
  3. Start drafting the three- to five-page proposal 
  4. Quiz on Business Case fundamentals 

Week 3: Business Case Structure 

  1. Learn differences between proposal and business case 
  2. Submit the proposal 
  3. Study the business case sections (problem, analysis, alternatives, ROI, risks, etc.) 

Week 4: Details of the Business Case 

Deep dive into: 

  1. Executive summary 
  2. Background 
  3. Detailed analysis 
  4. Alternatives 
  5. Goals / deliverables 
  6. Resources 
  7. Risks 

Week 5: Stakeholder Analysis 

  1. Identify stakeholders 
  2. Use SIPOC, RACI, political landscape tools 
  3. Learn stakeholder-mapping and action-planning 

Week 6: Detailed Analysis 

  1. Research-driven examination of the “current state” 
  2. Use internal data, literature, industry research, and trends 
  3. Draft Stakeholder Support Matrix 

Week 7: Value in Projects 

  1. Learn ROI, financial measures, FTE, cost estimates 
  2. Discussion post on how your organization tracks FTE value 

Week 8: Change Management 

  1. ADKAR, Kotter, Lewin, resistance, planned vs. emergent change 
  2. Discussion: Stakeholder communication 

Week 9: Alternatives 

  1. 9 dots puzzle, constraints, RAID model, cost & complexity of alternatives 
  2. Continue building business case 

Week 10: Cost/Benefit/ROI 

  1. Incremental revenue, hard vs. soft savings, cost avoidance, implementation costs 

Week 11: Risks & Issues 

  1. PESTLE, risk registers, issue logs, risk responses 

Week 12: High-Level Project Planning 

  1. Gantt chart basics 
  2. Right to left planning 
  3. Estimating time and scope 

Week 13: Recommendations 

  1. Writing a compelling recommendation 
  2. Choosing among alternatives 
  3. Connecting the recommendation to problem, goals, and analysis 

Week 14: Executive Summary 

  1. Write the Executive Summary last 
  2. Create strong openings, key findings, benefits/impact, and call to action 

Week 15: Rule of Three + Final Business Case Due 

Learn persuasive structure: 

  1. Tell them what you're going to tell them 
  2. Tell them 
  3. Tell them what you told them 
  4. Submit the full business case (up to 50 pages) 

Week 16: Next Steps + Final Presentation Deck 

  1. Create and submit the 20-slide pitch deck 
  2. Reflect on tools, frameworks, and your project journey
