The Proposal

A Project Proposal

The project proposal outlines the purpose, objectives, and high-level approach for engaging stakeholders and informing decision-making.

Project Proposal Overview

A short three- to five-page document that outlines a project, its details, objectives, and approach at a high level.

  • Executive summary
  • Basic background information
  • High-level objectives
  • Scope
  • Budget estimates
  • Proposed timeline
  • Expected risks
  • Key deliverables
  • Success criteria

Its purpose is to engage with the key stakeholder and gain their buy-in.

It answers the basic questions:

  • What is it?
  • What is it for?
  • Why is it important?
  • What do you need to get it done?
  • What do you get out of doing it?

Why a Proposal?

A proposal is an appetizer. It’s a sales brochure. It communicates what a sponsor needs to know in order to greenlight a project.

Don’t make it fancy. Make it clear, concise, and compelling.

The goal is to garner support for your idea so you can proceed to the business case.

If the proposal doesn’t lead to an agreement to begin the project, it has failed.
