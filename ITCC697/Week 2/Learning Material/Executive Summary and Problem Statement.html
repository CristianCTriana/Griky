Problem Statement

This section outlines the present circumstances as they exist at this time without attributing causes or proposing solutions.

  • This describes the current situation:
    • As it currently exists
    • Free of causes or solutions
  • Based on facts

Problem Statement Example

“The Data Ingestion Team must deliver data ready for use within 48 hours of receipt. Process analysis reveals that over 90% of the analysts’ time is spent on manual tasks related to fixing data hygiene issues (e.g., format and labeling) before analysis can begin. Time to review and validate the received data is under 10% and is performed at or near the deadline. Late-cycle data quality issues have resulted in overtime charges in seven out of eight data cycles.”
