The Proposal

Proposal Outline

Once you’ve sketched out your idea on the A3 and you have a good idea as to the nature of the problem and the applicability of your solution, you write it up in the proposal.

There’s a quote I once saw:

“Writing is nature’s way of letting you know how sloppy your thinking is.” —Richard Guindon

The proposal is a prose document that incorporates some of your analysis from the A3.

  • Executive Summary: What the proposal is for
  • Background: This is the current state and the why it should change
  • Solution: This is the future state, what needs to be done
  • Deliverables and goals: These are the outcomes and benefits
  • Resources: People, money, and time you think you need
  • Risks: What could go wrong
  • Conclusion: Selling the sizzle, the bang for your buck
