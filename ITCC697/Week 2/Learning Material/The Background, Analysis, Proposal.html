The Proposal

The Background, Analysis, Proposal

Background

This section is all about the current state. It situates the problem in a particular context, reiterates how things are currently going, and shows what the problems are on, as well as their impact.

  • Business or organizational context
  • Where things stand today
  • Details on problems

Analysis

In this section, you’re detailing what is wrong with the current state. Here you will answer questions like:

  • How long?
  • How much?
  • How many mistakes?
  • State the suspected root causes of the issue
  • Use numbers, facts, and data

This section shows what you know about the problem, how you know it’s a problem, and where you think the problem originates.

Proposal

This section is for you to state what your idea is.

  • What you plan to do about the problems
  • How the solution affects the problems
