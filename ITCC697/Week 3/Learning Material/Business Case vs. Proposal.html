The Business Case

Business Case vs. Proposal

A proposal is a short three- to five-page document that outlines a project, its driving needs, objectives, and approach at a high level. The business case is a much more in-depth document of no more than 50 pages that provides the detail and analysis. The table below highlights the differences.

Business case vs proposal
Business Case Proposal
~50 pages Three to five pages
Detailed High-level
Objective analysis Persuasive
Target: Internal stakeholders (e.g., leadership, sponsors) Target: internal or external stakeholders (sponsors, customers)
Includes objective analysis of costs, benefits, ROI, strategic fit Promotes proposed solution

Why a Business Case?

  • Every individual needs to know how to put forth an argument and back it up with evidence.
  • All organizations require justification for undertaking work.
  • Some organizations require formal business cases.
  • Everyone has ideas.
  • Few formalize these ideas.
  • A business case takes an idea, formalizes it, and marshals the evidence to support it in order to convince the decision-makers to get the organization to do it.
