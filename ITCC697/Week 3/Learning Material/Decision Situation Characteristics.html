The Business Case

Decision Situation Characteristics

Nature of the problem

  • What is the problem?
  • How do you know?
  • What data do we have?
  • What do we know to solve it?

Decision Environment

  • Market information
  • Organizational culture
  • Resource constraints (e.g., time, money)

Decision-Maker

  • Who decides?
  • What is their background?
  • What are their chief needs?

System or Solution

  • What functionality exists or is needed?
  • What data is available and what do you need to gather?
  • What diagnostic information do you have or need to develop?

Decision Support

  • Matching the problem in its environment with who can decide
  • Informing
  • Socializing
  • Teaching
  • Sensemaking vs. sensegiving
  • Seeing a problem
  • Sharing the problem
  • Proposing a solution
  • Gaining support
