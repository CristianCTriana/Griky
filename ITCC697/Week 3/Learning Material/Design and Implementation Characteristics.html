Design and Implementation Characteristics

Design Characteristics

When considering the design and implementation of a decision support solution, it is crucial to evaluate several key characteristics to ensure its effectiveness and compatibility within the organization. The following points outline important questions that should be addressed during the design phase:


  • Do we have a solution?
  • Do we have the technology?
  • How will the solution align with our existing process?
  • What is its uptime and availability?
  • Who uses it?
  • How much detail is required?

Implementation Characteristics

Several key factors contribute to the successful implementation of a new system or process. Top-down support, user buy-in, and the presence of champions are vital in driving adoption and overcoming resistance. Clear communication, solid development support, and thoughtful decisions—such as whether to buy or build—help streamline the transition. Comprehensive training, thorough testing, and a carefully managed rollout ensure operational readiness and lasting effectiveness.


  • Top-down support
  • User buy-in
  • Champions
  • Communication
  • Development support
  • Buy vs. build
  • Training
  • Testing
  • Rollout

Success of the System

Key criteria for system success are identified below. Evaluating these factors ensures lasting positive impact after implementation.


  • Technically sound
  • High sustained rate of adoption
  • Impact on cost, quality, and cycle time
  • Organizational impacts
    • FTE released
    • Burden diminished
    • Higher throughput
