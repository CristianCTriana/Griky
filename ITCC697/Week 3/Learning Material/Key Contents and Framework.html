The Business Case

Key Contents and Framework

Key Contents

A comprehensive document must answer the core functional questions:

Why

Details the reasons the project should be undertaken.

What

Details the changes needed to solve the problem.

How

What needs to be done, how long it will take, how much it will cost.

When

What will be delivered at what point with what tradeoffs.

Who

Details the team needed for delivering results.

Framework for Answering Those Questions

This is a framework for addressing the questions that come up during the project.

  • Demand for a solution
  • Supply of decision support
  • Matching that supply with the demand
  • Design characteristics of the system/solution
  • Characteristics of the implementation
  • Success criteria
