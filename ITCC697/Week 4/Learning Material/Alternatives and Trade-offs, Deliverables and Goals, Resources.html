Week 4
Details of the Business Case

Alternatives and Trade-offs, Deliverables and Goals, Resources

After proposing a solution, decision-makers expect to see evidence that you explored other options and considered feasibility. The sections on alternatives, deliverables, and resources demonstrate that your recommendation is thoughtful, realistic, and achievable within organizational constraints.

This section shows that you considered multiple ways to address the problem before selecting your recommended solution. You will describe viable alternatives, including doing nothing—and explain the trade-offs associated with each option. This comparison strengthens your recommendation by showing why your chosen solution is preferable.

  • What else might be done?
  • “Do nothing” is one alternative—at what cost?
  • “Buy vs. Build” is another—at what cost?
  • Offer different solutions:
    • Technical solution with an existing product
    • Technical solution with a new product
    • Training
    • Staffing
  • Menu option
  • Choose one from Column A, one from Column B

In this section, you will define what the project will produce and how success will be measured. Deliverables should be tied to short-, medium-, and long-term goals and linked directly to business outcomes such as cost savings, quality improvements, or cycle time reductions.

  • Given the proposed solution, what gets delivered when?
    • Short-term
    • Medium-term
    • Long-term
  • For a given deliverable, how does it contribute to the solution?
  • How long will it take to achieve the desired state?
  • Are there long-term and short-term deliverables?
  • What will success look like? Quantify it:
    • Cost impacts
    • Quality improvements
    • Cycle time reductions

Every proposal requires resources. This section identifies the time, money, and people needed to implement your solution. Clearly defining these resources helps decision-makers evaluate whether the project is realistic and sustainable.

  • TIME
    • How long will it take?
    • Is there a hard deadline?
  • MONEY
    • How much will it cost?
    • Will there be savings or profits that result?
  • PEOPLE
    • Who do you need?
    • What are their roles?
    • Who is impacted?
