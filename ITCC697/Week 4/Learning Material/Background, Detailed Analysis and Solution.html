Week 4
Details of the Business Case

Background, Detailed Analysis and Solution

Now that you understand what a business case includes, the next sections help you build the foundation for your proposal by clearly describing the current state and the business need.

Select each tab to learn more.

The Background section explains the current state of the organization or system and clearly defines the business need. In this section, you will expand on your problem statement by describing where the need exists, how it was identified, and why it matters. This context sets the stage for the analysis that follows.

  • An explanation of the current state
  • A clear statement of the business need
  • Where the need exists
  • How the need was determined
  • The proposal has a terse problem statement. Here, it is developed further.

In this section, you will analyze the business need in depth using data and evidence. This analysis should include relevant statistics, costs, quality issues, cycle time concerns, and trends drawn from scholarly sources and industry reports. The goal is to demonstrate, through evidence, that the current state is insufficient and that action is justified.

  • Relates to the current state
  • Includes data and statistics that provide dimension to the need
    • Cost
    • Quality
    • Cycle time
  • Analogous to a literature review in a thesis or dissertation
    • Scholarly articles
    • Industry reports
    • Competitive benchmarks
    • News items
    • Trend analysis

Based on your analysis, this section presents your recommended solution. You will describe what should be done, explain how it addresses the identified need, and justify why it is the best option. Your solution should be specific, aligned with business goals, and measurable when compared to the current state.

  • Given the detailed analysis, what should be done?
  • Begin with the big picture: Do this
  • Go into detail: Address this with that
  • Explain why it will work
  • Quantify the solution relative to the current state
  • Align it with the goals of the business
  • Discuss negative impacts that might ensue
