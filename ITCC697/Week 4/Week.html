| Week 4 Relevance: Details of the Business Case:

This lesson brings together everything you have been building toward in the course by showing how a business case functions as both an academic and professional decision-making tool. Each section of the business case—background, detailed analysis, solution, alternatives, resources, risk, and ROI—follows a logical progression that mirrors how real organizations evaluate whether a project is worth pursuing. You move from clearly defining a problem and analyzing the current state with evidence, to proposing and justifying a solution that aligns with organizational goals. In doing so, you apply research methodologies, integrate disciplinary knowledge, and critically evaluate sources, directly supporting the course outcomes and learning objectives tied to investigation, analysis, and scholarly critique.

These ideas matter because the business case is not just an assignment, but the primary assessment and culmination of the course, reflecting how decisions are made in professional IT and business contexts. By learning how each section connects—how data supports the need for change, how alternatives strengthen a recommendation, and how risks and ROI address leadership concerns—you develop skills that extend beyond the classroom. The emphasis on evidence, measurable outcomes, and strategic alignment prepares you to justify decisions, communicate value to stakeholders, and translate research into action. Ultimately, this lesson shows you why careful analysis and structured reasoning are essential for turning ideas into approved, implementable solutions.
