Stakeholder Analysis

How to Identify Your Stakeholders

Once stakeholders are broadly defined, the next challenge is identifying who they are in the context of your specific project. Stakeholders may be named individuals, defined roles, or representative groups. This section introduces two complementary approaches, personbased and personabased identification, to help ensure that no critical perspectives are overlooked. 

Person (Named) 

  • Manager 
  • Executive 
  • Peer 
  • Customer 
  • IT 
    • Developer 
    • Tester 
    • End user 

Persona (Exemplar) 

  • Management 
  • C suite 
  • Sales, Marketing, Operations 
  • Customer segment 
  • IT 
    • Development 
    • Testing 
    • Users
