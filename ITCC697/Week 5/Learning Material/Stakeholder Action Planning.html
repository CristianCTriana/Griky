Stakeholder Action Planning

Once you know where your stakeholders stand relative to your project, you can lay out a plan of action to engage them more effectively. Below is a simple matrix to capture this information:

A table showing a stakeholder action planning matrix with columns for: Stakeholder, Level of Support (As Is and Should Be), Type of Resistance, Need, and Method of Delivery. The stakeholder rows list Potter, Snape, and Weasley.

Action Plans:

  • Potter: "Strongly Supportive" both currently and desired. Need: Sustain support. Method of delivery: In-person communication.
  • Snape: Currently "Strongly Against" and should be "Neutral." Shows political resistance. Need: Encouragement. Method of delivery: Enlist Dumbledore and McGonigal to influence.
  • Weasley: Currently "Moderately Against" and should be "Moderately Supportive." Shows individual resistance. Need: Reassurance. Method of delivery: Daily feedback meetings.
