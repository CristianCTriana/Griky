Week 5 Stakeholder Analysis: Quick Assessment

Multiple Choice Questions 

Question 1: According to the modern view (Kujala et al., 2022), who qualifies as a stakeholder?

Correct answer: C. Anyone who affects or is affected by organizational activities

Feedback: The modern view expands the definition of stakeholders to include anyone impacted by organizational actions, not just traditional groups.

Question 2: What is the main purpose of using a SIPOC diagram in stakeholder analysis?

Correct answer: B. To identify suppliers, inputs, processes, outputs, and consumers

Feedback: SIPOC diagrams help map out key elements and identify stakeholders by their roles in the process.

Question 3: Which tool is often associated with project execution and focuses on responsibility and accountability?

Correct answer: C. RACI chart

Feedback: RACI charts assign roles for Responsibility, Accountability, Consultation, and Information in project management.

Briefly explain why stakeholder socialization and communication are important for the acceptance of a business case. 

Sample Answer: Socializing ideas and communicating with stakeholders builds support, addresses concerns, and increases the likelihood of approval. 

Feedback: Effective communication ensures stakeholders understand and are engaged in the project, which is key to gaining buy-in.
