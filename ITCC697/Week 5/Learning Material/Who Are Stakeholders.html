Who Are Stakeholders?

Before you can build support for a business case, you must understand who is affected by it. Stakeholders are not limited to customers or executives; they include anyone who can influence, or be influenced by, your project. This section introduces both the traditional and modern views of stakeholders and highlights how stakeholder definitions have expanded over time to reflect the complexity of today’s organizations.

Classic View

ca. 1930 (Brugha & Varvasovsky, 2000)

  • Customers
  • Employees
  • General Public
  • Shareholders

Modern View

ca. 2022 (Kujala et al., 2022)

Anyone (e.g., individuals, groups, or organizations) who affect or are affected by organizational activities.
