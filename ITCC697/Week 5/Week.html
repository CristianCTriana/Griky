According to Kujala et al. (2022), “Stakeholder engagement refers to the aims, activities, and impacts of stakeholder relations in a moral, strategic, and/or pragmatic manner” (p.1160).

This module is about the human side of the business case. It offers a view into identifying stakeholders and meeting their needs.

The human side of a business case is the most important, but often the most neglected. Communication and socialization of your ideas with the stakeholders will go a long way toward easing the approval process.

| Week 5 Relevance: Stakeholder Analysis:

Stakeholder analysis connects the human, analytical, and political dimensions of building a successful business case by helping project leaders understand who is affected, how they are affected, and why their perspectives matter. The Lesson begins by expanding the definition of stakeholders from a narrow, traditional view to a modern understanding that includes anyone who can influence or be influenced by organizational activity, emphasizing that projects do not exist in isolation. Tools such as person based and persona based identification, SIPOC diagrams, and RACI charts show how stakeholders can be systematically identified through roles, processes, and responsibilities rather than assumptions, reinforcing the importance of evidence based analysis in project planning.

These ideas matter because stakeholder support—or resistance—often determines whether a business case is approved, delayed, or rejected. By analyzing stakeholder positions, levels of support, and types of resistance, students learn how to move beyond technical solutions and address the social and political realities of organizations. The stakeholder analysis and action planning matrices demonstrate that stakeholder documents are not judgments, but strategic tools used to guide communication, build buy in, and increase the likelihood of success. Ultimately, the Lesson shows that effective socialization and communication of ideas are just as critical as analytical rigor, making stakeholder engagement a core competency for developing persuasive and implementable business cases.
