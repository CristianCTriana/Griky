Detailed Analysis of the Need

This section functions much like a literature review in a thesis or dissertation. Its purpose is to define the scope, scale, and impact of the problem using verifiable evidence. Students should move beyond anecdote to present measurable indicators related to cost, quality, cycle time, and performance, synthesizing data from scholarly, industry, and organizational sources.


  • Relates to the current state
  • Includes data and statistics that provide dimension to the need:
    • Cost
    • Quality
    • Cycle time
  • Analogous to a literature review in a thesis or dissertation:
    • Scholarly articles
    • Industry reports
    • Competitive benchmarks
    • News items
    • Trend analysis

Details, Details, Details

  • Restate the problem:
    • Details matter

  • State the facts as known:
    • Internal reports

  • Research dimensions:
