Scholarly research

Applied / Theoretical

Is the research primarily focused on practical applications (applied) or on developing broader theoretical understanding (theoretical)? For example, does it explore real-world solutions or foundational concepts?

Fundamental

Does the research address basic principles, core mechanisms, or underlying theories that explain the phenomena in question?

Qualitative / Quantitative

What research methods are being used? Are studies gathering non-numerical, descriptive data (qualitative), or are they analyzing numerical data and statistics (quantitative)? What insights do these methods provide?

Case studies

What notable case studies exist in this field, and what specific insights or lessons do they provide about the current state?

Reviews

Are there comprehensive literature reviews or meta-analyses that summarize the current state of knowledge? What do these reviews highlight as key trends or gaps?

Methods

What research methods or approaches are most commonly used in this area? How do these methods shape the findings and conclusions?
