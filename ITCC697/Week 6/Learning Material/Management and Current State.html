Management and Current State

What is your leadership saying?

What is your management telling you?

Use these questions to critically evaluate messaging from each communication channel listed below.

  • Public speeches: These often set the tone for organizational priorities and values.
  • Interviews: Interviews can reveal candid thoughts and clarify leadership perspectives on key issues.
  • Annual reports: Annual reports provide a comprehensive overview of performance and strategic direction.
  • Press releases: Press releases offer timely information about new initiatives, changes, or responses to industry events.
  • Office memos: Office memos can communicate internal policies, immediate actions, or operational updates directly to staff.
