Week 7
Value in Projects

Benefits

The Big Three

  • Cost: Less waste, energy, or labor
  • Quality: Smoother, faster processes; reduced defects; enhanced product
  • Cycle time: Reducing the time it takes to do the work

Ideal Benefits

  • Increased profits
  • Higher sales
  • More customers

Soft Benefits

  • Happy, engaged staff
  • Satisfied customers
  • Enhanced brand reputation
