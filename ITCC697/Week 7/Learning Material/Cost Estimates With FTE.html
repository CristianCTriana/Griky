Cost Estimates With FTE

Some companies associate pro forma salary bands to FTE in order to aid in estimates. (Your finance department and/or human resources department can provide you with details on this.)

This number can be used to calculate current cost or future estimated savings.

This image displays a four-column table that compares full-time equivalent (FTE) bands, annual salary, annual work hours, and calculated hourly rate.
