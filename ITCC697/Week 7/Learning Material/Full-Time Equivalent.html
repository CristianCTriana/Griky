Full-Time Equivalent

FTE is a unit of measurement often used to quantify hours worked.

Formula

FTE = Hours actually worked / Typical 40-hour week
  • Example: 1 FTE = 40 / 40
  • The figure typically used for an annualized FTE is
    (40 hr/wk × 52 wk) = 2,080 hr

Special Note

A Full-time equivalent is not the same as a Full Time Employee. In the U.S., the IRS states a full-time employee works at least 30 hours per week or 130 hours per month.
https://www.irs.gov/affordable-care-act/employers/identifying-full-time-employees.

Note: Your firm may use something different to account for FTE, or not.
