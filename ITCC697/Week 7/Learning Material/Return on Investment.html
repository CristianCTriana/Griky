Return on Investment

Return on investment is one of the leading measures of outcome in business. If we invest in this, how much can we expect to get back?

Simple Formula

((Estimate of financial benefit − estimate of change cost) / Estimate of change cost) × 100 = ROI

Example Calculation:

  • Project Cost: $10,000
  • Estimated Benefit: $15,000
($15,000 − $10,000) / $10,000 = 0.5 × 100 = 50% ROI
