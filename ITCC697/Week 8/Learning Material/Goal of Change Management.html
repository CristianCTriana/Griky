Change Management

Goal of Change Management

The goal of change management for the business case is to get stakeholders to adapt to the change.

Change management overall typically includes the following steps:

  1. Recognize the need to change
  2. Want to change
  3. Accept the change
  4. Use the change
  5. Sustain the change

For the business case, the focus is on the first two: recognition and desire.

Change Resistors

Change resistors are the attitudes, beliefs, or behaviors that make individuals or groups reluctant to accept or support organizational change. These resistors can stem from comfort with established routines, fear of the unknown, or concerns about how changes will impact existing relationships and systems. Understanding and identifying common sources of resistance is an important step in effective change management, as it enables organizations to address concerns proactively and facilitate a smoother transition for all stakeholders.
