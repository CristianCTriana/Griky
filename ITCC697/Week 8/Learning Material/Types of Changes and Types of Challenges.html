Change Management

Types of Changes and Types of Challenges

Types of Changes

Not all organizational changes are the same, and different types of change introduce different kinds of challenges. Distinguishing between these categories helps leaders anticipate resistance, select appropriate change management strategies, and avoid applying the wrong solution to the wrong problem.

Planned change

  • Top-down
  • Driven by formal objectives
  • Highly structured
  • Large scale

Emergent change

  • Bottom-up
  • Driven by changing conditions
  • Spontaneous and adaptive
  • Smaller scale

Types of Challenges

Challenges associated with change vary in complexity. Some can be solved using existing expertise, while others require learning, experimentation, and shifts in mindset.

Adaptive challenges

  • Less well-defined
  • Demand learning
  • Require experimentation
  • Peak resistance

Technical challenges

  • Clearly defined
  • Can be solved with what is known
  • Require execution
  • Relatively lower resistance

Key Takeaway: You can’t solve adaptive issues with technical solutions. Sometimes, technical solutions cause adaptive challenges.

Change Control

Change control is different than change management. Change control involves a rigorous process that carefully manages the administrative details, including:

  • What gets changed
  • Why it was changed
  • Who agreed to a change
  • When the change was approved
  • Issues related to:
    • Size, Scope, Scale, Configuration, Structure
  • Particular
