Resistance

Very often, the boxes you think are around your nine dots aren’t there. Some of the boxes around change are listed here:

Habit

“This is the way we do things around here.”

Complacency

“It works well enough now, don’t break it.”

Entrenched management

“The VP of that division and I have a long history.”

Outdated technology

“Our system has grown organically and is highly customized.”
