ITMG624 Course Overview

Scope, Learning Objectives, and Assessment

| Course Scope

This course provides students with a strong foundation for effective project management in modern organizations. It begins with a comprehensive introduction to the core components of the project management discipline, including an in-depth examination of the five project management process groups, which builds the knowledge necessary to oversee critical projects. Students will explore the challenges of initiating a project and developing strategic planning approaches to successfully begin the project management cycle. They will then apply these concepts to create practical project management plans used in real-world business environments. The course culminates in the development of a detailed final project, incorporating key concepts such as scope management, risk management, resource management, and schedule development.

| Program Learning Outcomes

Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) serve as a roadmap for educational success and guide the courses that you take.

This course supports the following PLOs:

  • Apply key management theory, principles, best practices, tools, and technologies associated with information systems.
  • Utilize graduate level critical thinking, reasoning, and writing to defend the logic and the conclusions in scholarly research.
  • Plan the processes, phases, domains, and measures for effectively managing complex information technology projects.
  • Assess the vulnerabilities of information systems with respect to security and the methodologies to mitigate this risk.
