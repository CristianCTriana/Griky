Week 1
Project Management and Project Work

Conclusions

Week 1 established the foundation for understanding project management as both a discipline and a practical skillset. This week’s focus on how projects progress from initiation to closure helped connect the importance of key elements such as risk, scope, and scheduling to each project phase. Through the readings and discussions, it became clear how these course objectives directly support real-world project success. Overall, Week 1 provided a strong starting point by reinforcing the core principles, success criteria, and collaborative practices that will guide effective project management throughout the course.
