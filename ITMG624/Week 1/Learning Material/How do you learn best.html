How do you learn best?

I want to encourage everyone to read the assigned textbook since it provides the core framework and terminology that you will rely on throughout your discussions and assignments. At the same time, I understand that everyone learns differently. Many students benefit from additional explanations or visuals, and resources like YouTube can offer some excellent tutorials.


If you choose to use these, please make sure you are looking specifically for materials that reference the 8th edition of the PMBOK since earlier editions have significant differences.

I also fully support the use of outside academic sources to deepen your understanding, if those sources are peer-reviewed and credible. The APUS/AMU library is an outstanding place to start and contains a wide range of scholarly articles, journals, and research tools that can reinforce the concepts you cover each week. Making use of these resources will strengthen your learning experience and help you approach the material with greater confidence and clarity.
