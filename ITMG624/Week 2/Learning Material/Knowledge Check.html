Week 2
Attributes of IT Project Management and Portfolio Management

Week 2 - Knowledge Check

Pause to check your understanding. This is not graded.

Question 1: IT project management primarily focuses on delivering specific outputs and outcomes within defined constraints such as scope, schedule, and cost.

Correct answer: A. True

Explanation: IT project management is focused on delivering defined outputs and outcomes while balancing constraints such as scope, schedule, cost, and quality. Project managers concentrate on executing specific work efficiently and effectively to meet agreed-upon objectives within these constraints.

Question 2: Portfolio management is concerned with managing interdependent project tasks to ensure a project meets its objectives.

Correct answer: B. False

Explanation: Portfolio management does not manage interdependent tasks within a single project. That responsibility belongs to project management and program management. Instead, portfolio management oversees a collection of projects and programs, prioritizing and selecting them to ensure alignment with organizational strategy and optimal use of resources.

Question 3: According to the PMBOK® (8th edition), portfolio management emphasizes aligning projects and programs with an organization’s strategic goals.

Correct answer: A. True

Explanation: According to the PMBOK® (8th edition), portfolio management emphasizes aligning projects and programs with an organization’s strategic goals. Its primary purpose is to ensure that the work being undertaken collectively supports the mission, vision, and long-term objectives of the organization.

Question 4: Project managers and portfolio managers typically make decisions using the same criteria and at the same organizational level.

Correct answer: B. False

Explanation: Project managers and portfolio managers operate at different organizational levels and use different decision-making criteria. Project managers focus on tactical decisions related to delivering project outcomes, while portfolio managers make strategic decisions about which initiatives to invest in based on value, risk, and alignment with organizational priorities.

Question 5: Effective portfolio management helps organizations prioritize IT projects based on strategic value, risk, and resource availability.

Correct answer: A. True

Explanation: Effective portfolio management enables organizations to prioritize IT projects by evaluating strategic value, risk, and resource availability. This approach ensures that limited resources are allocated to initiatives that provide the greatest benefit and best support the organization’s strategic framework.
