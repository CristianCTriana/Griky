What to Expect in Week 2

In this module, you will explore how projects create value and deliver meaningful outcomes within the broader framework of organizational governance and strategy. The lesson emphasizes the importance of aligning projects with an organization’s mission, vision, and objectives to ensure that every effort contributes to long-term success. This subject was selected to help you understand how individual project activities fit into the bigger picture of organizational performance. By the end of the module, you will develop a systems-thinking perspective, aligning projects with an organization’s mission, vision, and objectives.

A real-world example of how alignment with organizational strategy affects project success can be seen in a healthcare organization that launched a new patient scheduling system. While the project team delivered the system on time and within budget, it failed to support the organization’s strategic goal of improving patient experience because it focused more on internal efficiency than ease of use for patients. As a result, adoption was low, staff created workarounds, and leadership eventually had to invest additional resources to redesign the system. This shows that even a well-managed project can fall short if it does not clearly align with the organization’s broader mission and strategic objectives.

This week’s focus highlights the difference between “doing projects right” and “doing the right projects.” Project management is about executing a project effectively and meeting scope, schedule, cost, and quality expectations. Portfolio management, on the other hand, focuses on selecting and prioritizing projects that best support organizational strategy and long-term goals. Understanding this distinction helps ensure that effort and resources are not only managed well but also invested in initiatives that truly move the organization forward
