Week 3 Deconstruct the Key Elements of a Project Charter and Statement of Work (SOW)

Completing this week's case study will allow you to develop and broaden your growth mindset skills. Apply some of the techniques from the activities of this lesson to progress your knowledge and skills beyond what you gain from this course. Implementing a plan for a project and writing a project charter and a statement of work are all activities that will help advance your growth mindset skills, as these activities require you to create project goals, scopes, and deliverables. A growth mindset is often the expectation of a practitioner who is aiming to develop professional skills for future endeavors. Other aspects of the activities for this week include managing a project life cycle, breaking a project into manageable tasks, and anticipating dependencies. Again, these are the skills that would heighten your ability to strategically plan and adapt tactical and operational goals to address your strategies. Continue to develop mitigation strategies, practice your fortitude and problem-solving capabilities, and reflect on and refine your experiences as you cultivate a growth mindset.
