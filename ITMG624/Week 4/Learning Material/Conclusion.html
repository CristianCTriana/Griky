Assessing Organizational Strategy

Conclusions

Strategic management is a critical activity that enables an organization to succeed.

Strategic management also is a complex process that a variety of other managerial actions must support. For example, as this lesson has explained, strategic management depends on activities such as the development of an organization’s vision and mission statements.

Developing strategies and practicing strategic management are challenging, but they are also necessary to enable any organization to succeed.
