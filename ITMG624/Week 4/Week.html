What to Expect in Week 4

This week’s material emphasizes that project management requires tailoring approaches based on context, stakeholder expectations, and environmental factors. You learn how the eight domains interact continuously rather than following a strict linear sequence, reinforcing the importance of systems thinking and adaptability. Ultimately, this module helps you develop a holistic, outcome-oriented mindset that reflects the complexity of real-world project environments.

| Understanding Your Text

As you read PMBOK 8th ed. Ch. 2.2, focus on how the WBS supports risk identification and management. Take notes on the steps for linking risks to specific work packages and consider how these processes interact in your own work or study context.

Objectives

CO-4: Analyze processes of project risk management with Work Breakdown Structure (WBS). ▼
  • LO-4.1: Explain the use of the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) to identify and categorize potential project risks during the planning phase.
  • LO-4.2: Assess the relationship between project risk management processes and WBS development to determine strategies for minimizing risk impact on deliverables.
