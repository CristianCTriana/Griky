Week 5 Processes of Realistic Scheduling and Estimation Accuracy

Building digital literacy skills is vital for successfully completing this week’s lesson. Developing your digital literacy skills will enable you as a graduate student to critically engage with digital tools such as Microsoft Project, analyze scheduling processes, and evaluate estimation techniques with precision. By understanding the use and purposes of exercising sharp digital literacy techniques and skills, you can better assess the accuracy of project timelines and resource allocations, and conduct research affiliated with project management and scheduling. Apply what you learn in this course to real-world scenarios. Digital literacy skills will help you to synthesize project success factors, leverage applicable tools and matrices, and interpret data-driven insights from scholarly sources. These skills will improve your ability to produce well-researched, well-formatted documents and papers that reflect industry expectations.
