What to Expect in Week 1

Welcome to Week 1! This week, we will be building a foundation for our understanding of psychopathology. Topics include the systems by which mental disorders are defined (DSM and ICD), and how they have shaped the course of research and clinical practice, and informed expectations regarding treatment and prognosis. We will discuss major debates surrounding the nature of mental disorder, categorical versus dimensional models of psychopathology, as well as the evolution of diagnostic frameworks through history. The week will culminate with an overview of the role of culture in the definition of normality and distress, and the implications of this for the assessment and theorizing about psychopathology in today’s world.

Objectives

CO-1: Examine major theoretical and nosological approaches to defining and understanding psychopathology. ▼
  • LO-1.1: Explain the biological, psychological, sociocultural models of psychopathology.
CO-2: Compare current diagnostic systems. ▼
  • LO-2.1: Distinguish between the DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 diagnostic systems.
CO-4: Analyze ways in which culture, identity, and historical context can impact symptom presentation, explanatory models, help-seeking, and diagnosis. ▼
  • LO-4.1: Examine the influence of culture on concepts of normalcy, abnormality, and distress.
  • LO-4.2: Assess cultural syndromes in relation to diagnosis.
