Week 2

Assessment, Diagnosis, and Cultural Formulation

Week 2 - Knowledge Check

Question 1: A primary goal of psychiatric assessment is to:

Correct answer: B. Systematically gather information to support diagnostic formulation and intervention planning

Question 2: Effective diagnostic interviewing typically involves:

Correct answer: C. A balance of narrative exploration and standardized clinical probes

Question 3: The DSM-5-TR Cultural Formulation Interview (CFI) is designed to help clinicians:

Correct answer: C. Elicit patients’ explanatory models, cultural context, and illness meaning

Question 4: Which of the following domains is NOT a primary focus of the CFI?

Correct answer: D. Neurobiological etiology of symptoms

Question 5: Cross-cultural measurement validity requires attention to:

Correct answer: B. Linguistic, conceptual, and metric equivalence across groups

Question 6: From the options below, which would be an ethical concern most likely to present when utilizing Western screen tools with culturally diverse populations?

Correct answer: A. Over-pathologizing culturally normative distress

Question 7: Research on diagnostic bias suggests clinicians are more likely to:

Correct answer: C. Rely on confirmatory information that supports initial impressions

Question 8: Clinicians using the CFI frequently report that:

Correct answer: B. It deepens cultural understanding and improves diagnostic accuracy

Question 9: From a cultural formulation perspective, standardized assessment tools risk reduced validity when:

Correct answer: A. Concepts measured lack cultural equivalence

Question 10: Addressing stigma in cross-cultural diagnostic practice is important because stigma can:

Correct answer: C. Influence symptom reporting, help-seeking, and treatment engagement
