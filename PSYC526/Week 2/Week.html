What to Expect in Week 2

Hello and welcome to Week 2! This week introduces the core components of psychiatric assessment and diagnostic interviewing, including how clinicians gather clinical history, evaluate symptoms, and formulate diagnoses. The week emphasizes cultural responsiveness through tools such as the DSM-5-TR Cultural Formulation Interview and explores how bias, stigma, and structural barriers influence assessment accuracy and access to care.

Objectives

CO-2: Compare current diagnostic systems. ▼
  • LO-2.2: Use the DSM-5-TR Cultural Formulation Interview (CFI) with case material.
CO-3: Examine culturally informed assessment and diagnostic formulation. ▼
  • LO-3.1: Evaluate assessment tools for cultural validity and bias.
  • LO-3.2: Examine a culturally informed diagnostic interview.
CO-7: Evaluate ethical concerns in the field of psychopathology, including stigma, systemic inequities, and barriers to care with a focus on cultural humility and social justice. ▼
  • LO-7.1: Identify ethical considerations in cross-cultural diagnosis.
