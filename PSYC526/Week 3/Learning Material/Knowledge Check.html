Week 3 - Knowledge Check

Question 1: Anxiety disorders are observed across cultural groups, but the dominant symptom forms (e.g., somatic vs. cognitive) vary based on cultural norms and explanatory models.

Correct answer: True

Question 2: nic disorder and culture-linked panic syndromes such as khyâl attacks represent identical psychiatric constructs and are considered equivalent across diagnostic systems.

Correct answer: False

Question 3: Trauma- and stressor-related disorders involve dysregulation in fear and stress response systems, including the HPA axis.

Correct answer: True

Question 4: Adjustment Disorder and PTSD are distinguished based on the type of stressor involved instead of the time course of symptoms.

Correct answer: False

Question 5: Cultural idioms of distress, such as ataques de nervios, often arise in response to interpersonal stressors and may not map neatly onto DSM-5-TR anxiety categories.

Correct answer: True

Question 6: Migration stress and displacement can create risk environments for anxiety and trauma-related disorders, even in the absence of a singular traumatic event.

Correct answer: True 

Question 7: Historical trauma describes collective and intergenerational patterns of distress linked to large-scale traumatic events such as colonization, genocide, and forced relocation.

Correct answer: True
