What to Expect in Week 3

Welcome to Week 3! This week, we will cover anxiety disorders and trauma- and stressor-related disorders, discussing their central clinical features, shared vulnerability processes, and dimensional overlap. We will learn about temperament and personality-based risk models, neurobiological pathways, and current theoretical debates between categorical versus dimensional conceptualizations of anxiety. Cross-cultural variation in symptom expression and culturally specific trauma responses is highlighted, as is the role of sociocultural, historical, and structural context in shaping distress, diagnosis, and resilience.

Objectives

CO-4: Analyze ways in which culture, identity, and historical context can impact symptom presentation, explanatory models, help-seeking, and diagnosis. ▼
  • LO-4.3: Evaluate culture-bound syndromes related to fear, panic, and trauma.
CO-5: Summarize cross-cultural and global research on mental disorders, including culture-specific syndromes and idioms of distress. ▼
  • LO-5.1: Identify diagnostic criteria and mechanisms of anxiety and trauma disorders.
CO-6: Apply key concepts from psychopathology to case material to formulate biopsychosocial-cultural conceptualizations and differential diagnoses. ▼
  • LO-6.1: Distinguish cross-cultural differences in anxiety presentations (e.g., somatic vs. cognitive symptoms)
CO-7: Evaluate ethical concerns in the field of psychopathology, including stigma, systemic inequities, and barriers to care with a focus on cultural humility and social justice. ▼
  • LO-7.2: Examine the impact of historical trauma, migration, and displacement on psychopathology.
