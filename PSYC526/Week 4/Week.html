What to Expect in Week 4

Welcome to Week 4! This week, we learn about the symptom profiles, developmental courses, and etiological models associated with major depressive disorder, persistent depressive disorder, and bipolar spectrum disorders, including the neurobiological, cognitive, and interpersonal processes and mechanisms that have been shown to underlie mood dysfunction. This week also emphasizes cultural, contextual, and structural factors that impact the experience and expression of symptoms, help-seeking behaviors, and risk for suicide as well as the importance of engaging in culturally responsive and informed assessment and conceptualization for all clients.

Objectives

CO-1: Examine major theoretical and nosological approaches to defining and understanding psychopathology. ▼
  • LO-1.2: Describe the diagnostic features and etiological models of depressive and bipolar disorders.
CO-4: Analyze ways in which culture, identity, and historical context can impact symptom -presentation, explanatory models, help-seeking, and diagnosis. ▼
  • LO-4.4: Compare cross-cultural differences in emotion regulation and mood expression.
CO-5: Summarize cross-cultural and global research on mental disorders, including culture-specific syndromes and idioms of distress. ▼
  • LO-5.2: Assess stigma and cultural barriers to the treatment of mood disorders.
  • LO-5.3: Identify global risk factors and culturally specific protective factors for suicide.
