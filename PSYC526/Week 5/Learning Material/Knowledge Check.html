Week 5 - Knowledge Check

To answer this week’s knowledge check, you need to first review the case vignette below.

Case Vignette

Amira is a 23-year-old woman who is a recent immigrant to the United States from Morocco, where she completed her undergraduate education. She recently began graduate school at a local university. For the past three months, Amira’s roommates have observed changes in her speech, an increase in isolation, and descriptions of “messages” she receives through music and dreams. When asked in passing, Amira has explained that the messages are not voices or explicit commands but “signals” sent to help her make sense of events in her life. Amira denies hearing voices but reports intermittent feelings that she is being led or warned in the context of stressors. Amira notes that in her family and community culture, people often use dreams and other symbolic signs for the purpose of meaning-making, and that this is not considered pathological. However, her professors have noticed a decline in her academic performance over the past few months, missed paper deadlines, and some difficulty following complex exchanges in class discussions. She denies any use of substances, mania, or depressive symptoms. Amira indicates that the experiences have been gradually progressive, beginning shortly after she moved and increasing in frequency over the semester in ways that are more impairing to her functioning. Her affect during the interview was constricted, and her manner of relating was guarded (but not overtly hostile), her thought content was coherent but occasionally circumstantial, and she had mild cognitive difficulty with working memory tasks. She noted some stress related to acculturative pressure and homesickness but has not sought support outside of her friend group.

Question 1: From the provided case vignette above, which symptom cluster aligns most closely with the schizophrenia spectrum?

Correct answer: B. Negative symptoms and cognitive vulnerabilities

Question 2: The cultural meaning Amira assigns to “messages” received in dreams reflects which concept from Week 5 content?

Correct answer: C. Cultural explanatory models

Question 3: Which diagnosis would be least likely given the timeline of symptom emergence and absence of a discrete episode?

Correct answer: A. Brief psychotic disorder

Question 4: What is the most relevant element when differentiating between schizotypal and schizophrenia in this case?

Correct answer: D. Duration and trajectory of symptoms

Question 5: Amira’s description of messages as symbolic or metaphoric rather than commanding suggests which phenomenological distinction?

Correct answer: A. Ideas of reference rather than delusions

Question 6: Declining academic functioning and impaired working memory most directly reflect which research finding from Week 5?

Correct answer: B. Cognitive deficits are core features of schizophrenia-spectrum disorders

Question 7: When evaluating “odd beliefs” within cross-cultural assessment, culturally responsive psychopathology requires determining whether such beliefs are:

Correct answer: C. Idiosyncratic within the individual’s cultural group and associated with impairment

Question 8: The temporal association between migration stress and symptom escalation in this vignette aligns most closely with Week 5 material on:

Correct answer: B. Social defeat and socio-environmental stress models
