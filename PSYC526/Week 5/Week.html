What to Expect in Week 5

Hello and welcome! This week, we focus on schizophrenia spectrum and other psychotic disorders. More specifically, we will learn about schizophrenia, brief psychotic disorder, schizotypal personality disorder, and schizoaffective disorder. The core symptom domains of these conditions will be discussed along with neurodevelopmental and cognitive vulnerabilities, course and prognosis, and emerging treatment approaches. A major theme of consideration involves the ways that cultural meaning systems, historical trauma, and social-structural conditions inform the experience, meaning-making, and outcomes of psychosis—and the need for integrative, culturally responsive conceptualizations that go beyond simple symptom checklists.

Objectives

CO-1: Examine major theoretical and nosological approaches to defining and understanding psychopathology. ▼
  • LO-1.3: Identify diagnostic criteria and subtypes of psychotic disorders.
CO-4: Analyze ways in which culture, identity, and historical context can impact symptom presentation, explanatory models, help-seeking, and diagnosis. ▼
  • LO-4.5: Describe biological, cognitive, and cultural models of hallucinations and delusions.
CO-5: Summarize cross-cultural and global research on mental disorders, including culture-specific syndromes and idioms of distress. ▼
  • LO-5.4: Contrast course and prognostic differences across nations and cultural contexts.
CO-6: Apply key concepts from psychopathology to case material to formulate biopsychosocial-cultural conceptualizations and differential diagnoses. ▼
  • LO-6.2: Examine psychotic symptoms in cultural and spiritual contexts.
