Case Vignette

Leila is a 27-year-old woman who recently immigrated to the U.S. from Jordan to pursue graduate business school. In class, she is quiet, highly deferential to authority, and waits to be called on before speaking. She reports being “emotionally aware but private” and has noted that she tries to “accommodate the other person so that relationships run smoothly” by avoiding the expression of direct needs. Professors report she seems hypersensitive to criticism and peers describe that she often declines social invitations due to “being a burden.”

Leila’s family places heavy emphasis on collectivistic values, filial obligation, and reputation within the extended family. She has noted feeling conflicted since coming to the U.S. between these expectations and what she has seen as norms around assertiveness and self-promotion. She does not report distress in her home context but is concerned that U.S. norms make her seem “weak,” “dependent,” or “anxious.” She denies trauma history, mood episodes, or substance use. She has no history of self-harm, impulsivity, or unstable relationships. She reports her interpersonal style “works well back home” but “does not translate here.”

Question 1: Leila’s interpersonal style illustrates which key idea from Week 6?

Correct answer: B. Cultural scripts shape norms for emotional expression and interpersonal behavior

Question 2: Based on cultural formulation principles, which question would be clinically relevant?

Correct answer: C. “How are assertiveness and dependency viewed in your family or cultural community?”

Question 3: In a dimensional (AMPD/ICD-11) framework, Leila’s concerns are best conceptualized as:

Correct answer: C. Trait detachment and negative affectivity affecting functioning in specific contexts

Question 4: If a categorical DSM-5-TR lens were applied too rigidly, Leila’s traits might be misinterpreted as:

Correct answer: B. AVPD

Question 5: The dimensional models emphasize impairment in functioning and maladaptive traits rather than fixed categories.

Correct answer: True

Question 6: Cross-cultural research suggests that traits resembling dependent or avoidant patterns may be adaptive in certain sociocultural contexts.

Correct answer: True

Question 7: Gender and culture influence not only symptom expression but also clinician perception and diagnostic tendencies.

Correct answer: True

Question 8: Lack of assertiveness in a U.S. context automatically indicates personality pathology.

Correct answer: False
