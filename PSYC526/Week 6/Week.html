What to Expect in Week 6

Welcome to Week 6! This week, we focus on personality disorders. More specifically, we will cover traditional categorical models of personality disorder, as well as new dimensional trait models (e.g., DSM-5 AMPD, ICD-11). We will learn about several key personality disorders (i.e., borderline, antisocial/psychopathic, narcissistic, avoidant, obsessive–compulsive), as well as developmental and etiological models linking personality disorder to temperament, attachment, neurobiology, trauma, and environment. We will also address the role of culture, gender, and structural context in the expression, interpretation, and pathologizing of personality patterns.
