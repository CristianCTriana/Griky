Neurodevelopmental Psychopathology

Week 7 - Knowledge Check

Mei is a 14-year-old second-generation Chinese American adolescent referred for difficulties in academics and isolation from peers. Teachers have noted that she is quiet, polite, and compliant but that she stares at the floor when spoken to and avoids eye contact during group activities. Mei excels in advanced math courses but performs poorly on assignments that require written responses, move quickly between tasks, or are time-sensitive. She stated that loud noises and crowded spaces during lunch cause her to feel “overwhelmed” and prefers consistency in routines.

Her parents believed that she was naturally reserved because she had been taught to value modesty, respect, and taking education seriously. They also shared that the idea of their daughter having a “disorder” made them uncomfortable. Teacher reports indicate that Mei’s written answers are often brief and literal and “lack elaboration.” She also rarely volunteers information in class. Psychoeducational testing indicated average-to-superior intellect but underperformed relative to expectations on timed writing and pragmatic language assessments. Mei denied feeling anxious but reported that social situations make her feel “confused” and that she “masks” when she needs to.

There were no developmental assessments conducted throughout childhood.

Question 1: Mei’s pattern of cognitive strengths and weaknesses, combined with her sensory sensitivities and social communication differences, is most consistent with:

Correct answer: B. Autism Spectrum Disorder

Question 2: Which feature of Mei’s presentation highlights a common pathway for late identification of ASD in girls?

Correct answer: C. Social camouflaging and masking

Question 3: The DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 differ in their treatment of co-occurring neurodevelopmental conditions such as ASD and ADHD. ICD-11:

Correct answer: C. Allows co-diagnosis and explicitly acknowledges overlap

Question 4: Mei’s parents’ explanatory model reflects the role of cultural scripts in shaping perceptions of:

Correct answer: D. All of the above

Question 5: Neurodiversity frameworks would conceptualize Mei’s difficulties primarily as:

Correct answer: C. A context-dependent mismatch between traits and environments

Question 6: Which structural factor contributes to delayed diagnosis of ASD in girls and racialized youth in the U.S.?

Correct answer: D. Both B and C
