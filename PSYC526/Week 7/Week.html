What to Expect in Week 7

Welcome to Week 7! This week, we will discuss neurodevelopmental psychopathology. Neurodevelopmental disorders like ADHD and autism reflect early emerging differences in attention, social cognition, sensory processing, and learning. Neurocognitive disorders, on the other hand, are acquired losses of previously developed cognition. New dimensional, developmental, and neurodiversity models emphasize that diagnosis, impairment, and adjustment are both neurally and socioculturally determined.
