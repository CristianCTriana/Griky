What to Expect in Week 8

Hello and welcome to our final week! This week pulls themes from across the course together, moving from descriptive psychopathology towards applied formulation and interdisciplinary thinking. We will consider how biological, psychological, social, and cultural systems dynamically interact to influence mental health and psychopathology. Topics related to symptom meaning, functional impairment, and help-seeking are revisited through a lens that prioritizes cultural humility, contextual case formulation, and ethical reflection.

Objectives

CO-6: Apply key concepts from psychopathology to case material to formulate biopsychosocial-cultural conceptualizations and differential diagnoses. ▼
  • LO-6.5: Integrate biological, psychological, social, and cultural factors into clinical formulations.
  • LO-6.6: Develop culturally responsive treatment plans.
CO-7: Evaluate ethical concerns in the field of psychopathology, including stigma, systemic inequities, and barriers to care with a focus on cultural humility and social justice. ▼
  • LO-7.4: Apply multicultural ethics to case examples.
  • LO-7.5: Evaluate controversies and future directions in psychopathology.
