Confidentiality, Mandated Preporting and HIPAA

Week 4 - Knowledge Check

Question: Select the logical sequence for Fisher’s six steps (as described): 
  1. Obtain informed consent before disclosures (and again when legal requests occur).
  2. Be knowledgeable about limits of confidentiality and legal challenges.
  3. Advise clients of limits and obtain agreement before therapy.
  4. Limit information disclosed to what is feasible.
  5. Avoid unnecessary breaches via policies, notes/records, and encouraging client self-advocacy.
  6. Discuss confidentiality broadly with clients, colleagues, students, and legal professionals.

Correct answer: B. 2, 3, 1, 4, 5, 6

Rationale: The model begins with practitioner knowledge (2), then advising clients and obtaining agreement (3), then obtaining informed consent for disclosures (1), limiting information disclosed (4), avoiding unnecessary breaches (5), and concluding with broader discussion and awareness-building (6).
