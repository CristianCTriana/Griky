Week 5 - Knowledge Check

Question: Based on the AAMFT Code of Ethics (2015) and best practices in family therapy:
Which TWO ethical issues are most pressing for Dr. Lopez to address immediately, and why? 

Select the best answer:

Correct answer: C and D

C. The mother’s private disclosure, because confidentiality in family therapy must consider the rights and well‑being of all family members and requires clarity about what can be kept secret.

Rationale:

In family therapy, confidentiality is complex because multiple clients are involved. According to the AAMFT Code of Ethics (2015), therapists must clarify from the outset how individual disclosures will be handled and must consider their ethical responsibility to all members of the system. 

Systems theory conceptualizes symptoms as rooted in family dynamics, so private information about one member can influence treatment for all. Fisher (2009) also emphasizes that clinicians in family therapy must avoid becoming the keeper of secrets, because withholding information can compromise neutrality, trust, and clinical effectiveness. 

In this scenario, the mother’s secret disclosure threatens: 

  • Therapeutic neutrality 
  • Transparency within the family system 
  • Clarity about confidentiality policy 

This makes issue C one of the most urgent ethical concerns. 

Her failure to consult with Eric’s individual therapist, because family therapists must collaborate with other professionals to ensure coordinated and ethical care.

Rationale: 

The AAMFT Code of Ethics requires clinicians to coordinate care when clients are simultaneously receiving services from multiple professionals. Family therapy should not proceed without consultation because: 

  • Treatment goals may conflict 
  • Clinicians may duplicate efforts or inadvertently undermine each other 
  • Risk factors (e.g., harm to self or others) may not be adequately communicated 
  • Systemic understanding requires integrating all perspectives on client well‑being
    Since Eric is already in individual therapy, Dr. Lopez is ethically obligated to communicate with his individual therapist before continuing family therapy. This makes D the second most urgent ethical concern.
