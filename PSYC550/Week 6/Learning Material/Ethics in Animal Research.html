Week 6 Ethical Issues in Human and Animal Research

Ethics in Animal Research

Psychologists have an ethical obligation to ensure that nonhuman animals are treated in a humane manner that ensures consideration of their health and comfort and minimizes discomfort, illness, and pain. 

Beauchamp and Degrazia (2020) suggest the ethical use of nonhuman animals requires 

empirically-supported scientific purpose or established theory,
evidence that the species selected is the most appropriate choice for the study, and
the absence of a scientifically-valid nonanimal research alternative.
