Human Research Protections

The HHS Office for Human Research Protections developed a training for Human Research Protection. The training can be found here:

Human Research Protection Foundational Training [webpage]

Title 45 Public Welfare Department of Health and Human Resources Part 46 Protection of Human Subjects (45 CFR 46) establishes the federal regulations for human subjects research. Practitioners should be knowledgeable of these federal regulations and the decision charts for determining when IRB review is necessary and if informed consent is needed. The decision-making charts can be found here:

Human Subject Regulations Decision Charts: 2018 Requirements [webpage]
