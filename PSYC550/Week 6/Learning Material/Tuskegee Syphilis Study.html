Week 6 Ethical Issues in Human and Animal Research

Tuskegee Syphilis Study

In the early 70s, the Associated Press published a story about a longitudinal study examining syphilis in untreated men, referred to as the Tuskegee Syphilis Study (Heller, 2017). This research was conducted in the 1930s by the U.S. Public Health Service. Participants were included poor Black men with little education who were deliberately misinformed about their diagnosis and not offered treatment. Although the article published 40 years after the onset of the study, societal reaction resulted in the termination of the study.
