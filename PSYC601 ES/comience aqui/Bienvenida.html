Welcome to PSYC601: Qualitative Methods in Social Science Research! In this graduate-level course, you’ll explore the diverse approaches that make qualitative research a powerful tool in social science.

We’ll dive into topics like research ethics, interviewing techniques, conversation and discourse analysis, content analysis, and unobtrusive measures.

By the end of this course, you’ll be equipped to apply these methods in both pure research and clinical assessment contexts. Our main objectives include critiquing research methods, applying ethical frameworks, implementing interviews, evaluating analytical frameworks, analyzing approaches, and assessing ethical considerations in ethnographic research.

Get ready to sharpen your research skills and gain practical insights that will elevate your work in social science.
