What to Expect in Week 1

The primary work for this course and the culmination of the graduate psychology program is completing a master's thesis.

Throughout the next 16 weeks, you will put the prospectus you developed in PSYC697 into action. You will analyze the data to test your hypothesis, and report and discuss your results and their implications. You will also complete your literature review and revise the chapters from your prospectus into chapters one and three of your thesis.

In the first few weeks of the course, you will work on revising your introduction and methodology chapters from future-tense to past-tense and preparing the data from your chosen data source for analysis. First, this week we will start by getting acquainted with your classmates and sharing a bit about your research topic area (and why you chose it), what you've learned from your literature review so far, and the hypothesis you developed in PSYC697 on this week's Discussion board.

Students are expected to work with their professor and must follow all guidance provided in the course, including submission of each required component of the master's thesis. Students should not expect to submit a final product at the end of the course without having completed each stage of the research process as outlined. Professors are not required to accept a thesis without this systematic, stepwise review process.

Objectives

CO-1: Construct a discipline-relevant research project with at least one specific, measurable, and directional hypothesis ▼
  • LO-1.1: Explain a relevant research problem and associated clear, testable hypotheses
  • LO-1.2: Develop a comprehensive literature review to support the research hypothesis and gaps in existing research
  • LO-1.3: Discuss the implications of research findings in the context of the existing literature and theoretical framework
CO-2: Apply the appropriate research design (methodology), archival database, and statistical analyses to effectively test the hypothesis. ▼
  • LO-2.1: Examine archival databases to gather quantitative data necessary for hypothesis testing
  • LO-2.2: Apply the statistical analysis that will be used to test the hypothesis
CO-5: Apply the Ethical Principles of the American Psychological Association ▼
  • LO-5.1: Develop strategies to ensure ethical compliance in the design and conduct of research
  • LO-5.2: Produce a completed thesis report consistent with ethical principles of the American Psychological Association
