Week 2
Accessing and Cleaning Data (Weeks 2 through 4)

Data Cleaning is a Process

There is a reason why we have built several weeks into this course just for data cleaning. While it sounds simple, it can be a tedious task that can take some time. There are times when you are likely to get frustrated with it and need to walk away, but don't give up! Taking the time to properly clean your data will save you a lot of headaches when you run your statistical analyses.
