Module 4: Reporting Your Results (Weeks 8 through 11)

Conclusion

As you finish this module, your main focus has been carefully and systematically developing the Results section of your thesis. During this module, you have gradually summarized your findings by gathering relevant data, interpreting statistical outputs, and presenting results clearly and objectively in line with APA standards. This part of your thesis work highlights accuracy, transparency, and ethical responsibility—reporting the results of your data analysis without bias and ensuring they match your approved methodology. By the end of this module, you need to finalize the results summary, which should show coherence, accuracy, and scholarly rigor, and obtain formal approval from your instructor before moving forward.

Your ongoing reflection and planning for your next steps have been equally important. Updating your action plan and documenting your progress in the discussion forum reinforce the step-by-step nature of the thesis process and help maintain steady forward momentum. This structured approach is deliberate; each part lays the groundwork for the next, and skipping assignments can weaken the research's integrity. Once you have an approved Results section, you'll be ready to move into the Discussion section, where interpretation, connection with the literature, and exploring implications will take center stage.
