Guidance for Writing the Results Chapter of Your Thesis

Guidance for Writing the Results Chapter of Your Thesis (drafted with help from Microsoft Copilot)

1. Organization and Structure

A. Logical Flow

Begin with descriptive statistics (e.g., frequency counts, means, standard deviations) to provide an overview of your sample and variables.

Follow with inferential statistics (e.g., t-tests, ANOVA) to address your research hypotheses.

Use subheadings (such as "Subject Demographics" or "Test Results") to group related findings and enhance clarity.

B. Alignment

Ensure each result directly addresses your research hypothesis - avoiding including unrelated data.

2. Reporting Statistical Results

  • Clearly present summary statistics for each variable (e.g., means, standard deviations, ranges).
  • Use tables or figures where appropriate to make complex data more accessible.
  • For each hypothesis test report:
    • The statistical test used (e.g., independent samples t-test, ANOVA)
    • Test statistics (e.g., t, F, χ² values)
    • Degrees of freedom
    • p-values and significance levels
    • Effect sizes, if relevant
  • Clearly indicate which results are statistically significant.
  • Report all findings accurately and transparently - do not manipulate data to fit your hypothesis.
  • Disclose all relevant results, including non-significant findings.
  • Incorporate tables, figures, or charts to summarize key findings and trends.
  • Ensure that all visual aids are clearly labeled, referenced in the text, and include descriptive captions.

3. Writing Style and Professionalism

Objectivity and Focus

Present results objectively - do not interpret, speculate, or comment on what the results mean.

Be concise and focused: include only data relevant to your hypothesis.

Language and Formatting

Use clear, precise language and define statistical terms as needed.

Follow APA formatting and citation style throughout.

Maintain a professional tone and ensure your writing is free of grammar and spelling errors.

4. Checklist Before Submission

Checklist Before Submission
Item Check
Have you included all relevant descriptive and inferential statistics?
Are all tables and figures clearly labeled and referenced?
Is the Results section free from interpretation or commentary?
Have you checked for clarity, conciseness, and professionalism?
Are all results directly related to your research hypothesis?
Have you followed APA style for all formatting citations?
