What to Expect in Week 5

In this module, you will draft Chapter Five of your master’s thesis, the Discussion. In this chapter, you will interpret the meaning of the results you have obtained from your statistical analysis and discuss the broader implications of your findings for the field of psychology. Unlike the Results chapter, which is very structured and exclusively data-driven, you have more latitude in the Discussion chapter to speculate about the implications of your work and how it might influence future lines of research.
