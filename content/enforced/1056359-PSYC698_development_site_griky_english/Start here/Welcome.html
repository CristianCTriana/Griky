PSYC698 Course Overview

Welcome to course

The primary work for this course and the culmination of the graduate Psychology program is completing a master's thesis.

You have completed PSYC697, where you progressed through a stepwise construction of a research question and hypothesis, selected an archival/secondary data source, developed your introduction and methodology chapters and put together a prospectus, and submitted for IRB approval/waiver. You also created an annotated bibliography and annotated outline to help organize your literature review.

Throughout the next 16 weeks, you will prepare your data for analysis, analyze the data, and report and discuss your results and their implications. You will also complete your literature review chapter and revise your chapters one and three from your prospectus to past-tense, putting it all together into your completed thesis project.

Students are expected to work with their professor and must follow all guidance provided in the course, including submissions of each required component of the master's thesis. Students should not expect to submit a final product at the end of the course without having completed each stage of the research process as outlined. Professors are not required to accept a thesis without this systematic, stepwise review process.

Important: Students are not to conduct any direct human subjects research for this course. Students completing this phase of their degree will receive substantial guidance and support from their instructor and classmates throughout the course.
